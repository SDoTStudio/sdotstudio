Pawjong Plus terms

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Pawjong Plus is an optional monthly subscription from sdotstudio. You can play campaign levels without purchasing a subscription.

What is included

While your subscription is active, Pawjong Plus unlocks all shop cosmetic tile faces, tile styles and scenes. No banner, interstitial or rewarded ads are shown. Extra benefits normally offered through rewarded videos can be claimed directly without watching an ad. Existing limits on individual rewards and helpers still apply.

Price and monthly renewal

The United States plan costs USD $2.99 per month. Google Play shows the price in your local currency before you confirm a purchase. Regional prices and taxes may differ.

Payment is charged to your Google Play account. The subscription renews automatically each month unless you cancel before the next renewal. Your store confirmation shows the applicable price and billing period.

Manage or cancel

Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops future renewals, and access continues until the current paid period ends. Refunds, revocation, cancellation with immediate effect or applicable law may end access earlier. Refund requests are handled under Google Play terms and applicable law.

Deleting the game or your Pawjong account does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel through Google Play to stop future renewals.

Access and restoration

Subscription cosmetic access ends when the subscription expires. Cosmetics you earned or validly purchased separately are not removed merely because your subscription ends.

Campaign levels are available offline. New unlocks, extra reward claims, purchases, restoring purchases and sharing require an internet connection. Already verified subscription cosmetics can be used offline until their saved subscription expiry. Permanently earned or validly purchased cosmetics remain available.

Restore access using the same Google Play account and the Restore Purchases option in the game. An internet connection is required to verify access. The store's confirmed purchase and subscription status determine your rights.
