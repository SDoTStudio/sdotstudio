Pawjong

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Pawjong, provided by sdotstudio, uses information when you play. For privacy questions, contact havychevy3@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

Pawjong saves progress, scores, coins, pets, unlocks, lives, settings and level-recovery information on your device. Local reminders may also store scheduling information. Device backup and migration services may keep separate copies.

Advertising

Pawjong uses Google Mobile Ads and Unity Ads through mediation. These providers may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain their services. Advertising partners may also process purchase or conversion history associated with their services. Processing depends on your choices, device settings and the ads shown.

Learn more in Google’s privacy policy and Unity’s game-player privacy policy. Their policies explain their retention practices and available privacy controls.

Your choices

Where available, use Privacy choices in the game’s Settings to review advertising consent. Your device also provides advertising and notification controls. Declining optional advertising consent does not necessarily stop processing needed to provide ads, security or fraud prevention.

Support, sharing and notifications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Avoid sending sensitive information. Email providers handle delivery and storage under their own policies.

Sharing uses your device’s share sheet and the app you choose. Optional reminders are local notifications and can be turned off in the game or device settings.

Sign-in and online features

You can play the campaign without an account. Online friend codes and daily leaderboards require sign-in with Google or an existing Pawjong email account. Firebase Authentication processes your account identifier and email address to secure your account. Google sign-in also uses basic profile information. If you sign in with email and password, Firebase Authentication processes those details to verify your account. Your password is not stored in your Pawjong game save. Pawjong does not request access to your Gmail messages or contacts.

Firebase Firestore stores your account identifier, friend code, referral reward records and acknowledgement history. If you submit a daily score, your chosen display name and completion time appear on the leaderboard. Do not use sensitive information as your display name. These services help us provide online features and prevent duplicate rewards. Campaign progress remains on your device; signing in does not provide cloud-save recovery.

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and in-app purchases are not active in this release. If that changes, we will update this policy. Firebase is provided by Google; see Firebase privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Local game information stays on your device until it is removed or replaced. Clearing app storage removes local progress; operating-system backups may remain separately. Advertising providers manage information they hold under their own policies. Online account and game-service records are kept while your account is active or until you request deletion. To request deletion of your Pawjong account and associated online information, visit Account deletion or email us. We verify ownership before processing requests. Limited records may be retained where required by law or necessary to investigate fraud or resolve disputes. Account deletion does not delete your Google account or email account, information held independently by advertising providers, or separate device backups. Contact us for questions about information you have sent directly to support.

Security

No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure. Please do not include passwords or payment-card information in support messages.

Children

Pawjong is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Contact sdotstudio at havychevy3@gmail.com for privacy questions.
